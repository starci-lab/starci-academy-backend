# Chatbot business definition

## Purpose
Turn source-observed Chatbot capabilities into an owner-reviewed product contract. The child leaves keep functional intent, non-functional constraints, operating rules, and acceptance separate so implementation evidence cannot silently approve business policy.

## Authority
Repository observations are candidate inputs only. Product ownership must approve actors, supported journeys, safety boundaries, measurable service expectations, provider ownership rules, and acceptance outcomes.

## Done when
Every required business child is approved against cited observations, with unresolved choices retained rather than inferred.
