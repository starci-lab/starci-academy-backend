# Actors

## Authorized business owner or module administrator

Identity: `module-administrator`

- Open and complete a private Setup revision
- Review gates and evidence
- Run sandbox tests
- Apply an immutable context version
- Configure write-only credentials
- Enable, pause or recover live operation

## Authorized support collaborator

Identity: `support-operator`

- Use the primary operations chat
- Inspect and act in the Support workbench
- Approve replies
- Assign and resolve tickets
- Take over or resume AI on a customer conversation

## External customer contacting the business through an enabled channel

Identity: `customer`

- Send a support message
- Receive only policy-eligible responses
- Request or receive human handoff
