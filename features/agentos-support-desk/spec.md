# AgentOS Support Desk business module

Business head: `73e332430f69f4f2ec8029e68540ce9d012ffa5350ab354e9e3327ab64ebc51f`

## Mission

Support Desk is a durable customer-support capability that learns one business through private Setup revisions, proves and applies immutable context, receives live channel messages, always produces a policy-safe response or explicit delivery failure, preserves attributable customer history, queues evidence-backed work, and collaborates with operators through a separate operations chat and workbench.

## Lifecycle

1. **Teach Support Desk through a private guided revision:** The module has no applied context or an administrator chooses New setup revision.
1. **Prove, apply and separately enable Support Desk:** A Setup candidate is review-ready.
1. **Handle one inbound customer turn safely:** A verified enabled channel delivers an inbound event.
1. **Operate Support Desk through internal collaboration:** An authorized support operator opens Operate.
1. **Create and apply a later Support Desk context version:** The administrator needs to change business behavior.
1. **Fail safely and recover one affected support axis:** Knowledge, credential, channel, policy or provider readiness fails; an AI prompt-cache miss alone is normal and never degrades the module.

## Setup gates

The required gates are identity and offers, customer segments, intent taxonomy, permitted answers, prohibited claims, escalation and handoff, hours and SLA, tone and language, privacy and consent, channel behavior, knowledge readiness and operating mode.

## AI prompt/token cache boundary

The deterministic stable prefix is ordered as trusted system policy, Support Desk kind contract, versioned tool and widget schemas, Nivo bootstrap knowledge and active immutable business context. Current permissions, operating mode, credential readiness, customer, conversation, ticket, consent, takeover, SLA, retrieved passages and the current message occur after the prompt-cache boundary. Provider caching is an optional optimization; cache misses preserve the same governed result.

## Approval-bound proposals

- BR-SD-05: A module has at most one open Setup draft at a time.
- BR-SD-09: The required gates are identity and offers, customer segments, intent taxonomy, permitted answers, prohibited claims, escalation and handoff, hours and SLA, tone and language, privacy and consent, channel behavior, knowledge readiness and operating mode.
- BR-SD-13: Enable Live is separate from Apply and requires active context, knowledge readiness, policy readiness, verified channel and valid credential.
- BR-SD-15: Webhook verification material is Nivo-managed and never exposed to the language model.
- BR-SD-21: One designated primary operations Execute session receives proactive notices; optional additional Execute sessions remain collaborative only.
- BR-SD-23: Proactive notices obey recipient, quiet-hour, deduplication, acknowledgement and escalation policy.
