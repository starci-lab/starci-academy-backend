# Business contracts

## Eligibility

- Read: authenticated active academy member unless chat-banned.
- Write: authenticated active academy member unless muted or chat-banned.
- Guest: not eligible.

## Ownership

- A member may edit or remove only their own message.
- An edit remains visibly marked.
- Removal preserves an auditable record.

## Safety

- Reporting is confidential.
- A report hides the content for the reporter without deciding guilt.
- Moderation actions require a reason.
- Moderation evidence remains available for the approved retention period.

## Product boundary

Global Chat does not own direct messaging or canonical course Q&A.
