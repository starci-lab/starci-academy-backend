# Rules

1. Global Chat is one academy-wide public-to-members room.
2. Eligibility and chat sanctions are enforced at every read and write operation.
3. MVP content is text, emoji, reactions, replies, mentions, and safe links.
4. File, image, audio, video, voice, and live calls are out of MVP.
5. Authors can edit or remove only their own messages, and edits are marked.
6. Removal hides content from ordinary members while preserving an audit record.
7. Reports are confidential and do not reveal reporter identity to the reported member.
8. Reported content is immediately hidden for the reporter while remaining independently reviewable.
9. Moderators record a reason for removal, mute, ban, restoration, or dismissal.
10. Room notifications are in-app and user-mutable; email and push are out of MVP.
11. Spam, flooding, impersonation, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, doxxing, malware, and illegal content are prohibited.
12. Canonical course Q&A remains owned by `course-learning`.
13. Production launch requires an approved moderation-evidence retention policy.
