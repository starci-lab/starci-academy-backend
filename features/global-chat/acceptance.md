# Acceptance

- An active authenticated member can read recent history, post text, and have another eligible member observe the same visible message.
- A guest or inactive member cannot read or post.
- Members can reply, react, mention, edit, and remove only content they own.
- Unread position and mention indicators advance predictably when the member reads the room.
- A report creates one confidential moderation case and hides the reported content for the reporter without deciding guilt.
- A moderator can dismiss, remove, mute, ban, escalate, and record a reason.
- Muted and banned states are consistently enforced.
- Moderator removal and restoration preserve an audit trail.
- MVP exposes no direct messages, member-created rooms, media uploads, voice/video, or external notifications.
- Production launch stays blocked until the declared policy and operating gates are approved.
