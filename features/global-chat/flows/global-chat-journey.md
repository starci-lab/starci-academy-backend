# Global Chat journey

1. An eligible member discovers Global Chat in persistent academy navigation.
2. The member opens the shared room and resumes from the last-read point.
3. The member reads, posts, replies, reacts, mentions, edits, or removes owned content.
4. The platform updates unread and mention indicators for eligible members.
5. A member may report content or another member; the content is hidden for that reporter.
6. A moderator reviews the confidential case and dismisses, removes, mutes, bans, or escalates with a reason.
7. A community admin reviews escalations and confirms or restores the outcome.
