Global Chat is the academy-wide community conversation layer for active authenticated members. It is distinct from direct messaging and from canonical course-specific Q&A.

The approved boundary covers one shared member room, text conversation, replies, reactions, mentions, unread state, reporting, moderation, chat sanctions, auditability, launch gates, and measurable community outcomes.

Open policy decisions remain explicit launch gates and are not implicit implementation authority.
