# Overview · Projects and career

## Purpose

Learners move through a course personal-project loop from roadmap to task, explicit repository and AI-grading intent, asynchronous evidence, revision and next-task handoff. Career destinations remain discoverable.

## Included

- Personal project roadmap, task and result route families
- Enrollment-owned repository URL, branch and private-token settings
- Learner-selected grading language and eligible AI model or Auto
- Queued, processing, failed and completed grading states
- Immutable attempt history, revision loop and next-task handoff
- Headhunting directory and company detail

## Excluded

- Challenge authoring and grading
- Academy-wide AI chat
- Public profile presentation of completed projects
- Team-project collaboration
- Job-posting administration and provider CRM behavior
