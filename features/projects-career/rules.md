# Business rules · Projects and career

The personal project roadmap exposes a deterministic next task, completion percentage/facts and milestone task navigation.

- Strength: `confirmed`

## BR-02

- Strength: `confirmed`
- Evidence: `EV-007`

- Strength: `confirmed`
- Evidence: `EV-005`, `EV-008`
