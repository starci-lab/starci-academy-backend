# Flow · Progress, profile and league

> ID: `progress-profile-journey` · Trigger: An authenticated learner opens the dashboard or a profile/league route.

| # | Actor | Surface | Action | Result |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `learner` | `learning-dashboard` | Open dashboard overview or another dashboard tab | Identity, learning, quest, streak, goals and community regions settle independently |
| 2 | `learner` | `learning-dashboard` | Claim a completed daily quest | Points are granted once for the day |
| 3 | `learner` | `public-profile` | Open a public profile or evidence subroute | Public learning, project, challenge and skill evidence is displayed |
| 4 | `learner` | `league-standing` | Choose weekly or global standing | Podium, viewer standing and ranked rows are displayed |

## Outcomes

- The learner can understand current progress, public evidence and comparative standing
