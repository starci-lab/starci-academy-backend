# Actors · Study library

## Learner (`learner`)

Can:

- Choose existing Study or the separate scored cloze assessment
- Review due flashcards or a selected deck
- Start, pause or resume a persisted study session
- Configure and complete a scored cloze assessment using a word bank
- Inspect results and continue into recommended study
- Browse foundation resources
- Open the course mind map

## StarCi Academy platform (`platform`)

Can:

- Expose due work, mastery and resumable work
- Persist and restore review sessions and cloze-valid assessment sessions
- Reject non-cloze cards from assessment playability and never substitute review behavior
- Record progress and completion without changing contract semantics
- Return performance and next-study evidence
- List foundation resources
