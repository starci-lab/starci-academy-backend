# Business rules · Study library

- `BR-01` — Study and Quick quiz are explicit sibling modes and never collapse into one ambiguous session.
- `BR-02` — A review or quiz session is persisted before navigation to focused work.
- `BR-03` — Live work restores the persisted session identity, card order and acknowledged progress.
- `BR-04` — Leaving unfinished work preserves resumability; finishing early requires confirmation and reports only completed work.
- `BR-05` — A live session route represents in-progress work and a dedicated result route represents completed work.
- `BR-06` — Invalid, missing or expired sessions recover to a safe setup or overview with an explanation.
- `BR-07` — Scheduling, scoring, access gates and route identity remain defined by existing contracts.
- `BR-08` — Results prioritize weak-topic and contextual next-study actions over decorative celebration.
- `BR-09` — Every remote-data surface exposes pending, ready, empty, failed and retry or recovery behavior.
- `BR-10` — Foundation browsing preserves its existing paginated pending, ready, empty, failed and partial behavior.

All rules are `confirmed`. Flashcard expansion is evidenced by `EV-010` and reference evidence `EV-011`; existing source-backed behavior retains its original evidence IDs.
